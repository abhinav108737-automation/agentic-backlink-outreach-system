# Weekly Reporting & ROI

Executive report: discovered, qualified, rejected/reasons, contacts, outreach, follow-ups, response rate, positive rate, negotiations, guest-post agreements/submissions, published/verified backlinks, quality distribution, time-to-backlink, hours avoided, cost/backlink, campaign ROI indicators, SEO indicators, exceptions, optimization proposals, next-week pipeline.

Operational breakdown: campaign, source/search strategy, agent, contact type, domain category, message/template/prompt version, follow-up step, policy version, experiment cohort.

ROI:
BuildCost = engineering hours × loaded internal hourly cost + setup cost.
OperatingCost = human oversight + local infra + approved provider costs.
LaborSavings = avoided manual hours × loaded hourly cost.
AcquisitionSavings = reduction in cost per verified backlink × backlinks acquired.
SEOValue = configurable estimate from incremental visibility/traffic/leads.
ROI = (LaborSavings + AcquisitionSavings + SEOValue - BuildCost - OperatingCost) / (BuildCost + OperatingCost).
PaybackMonths = BuildCost / MonthlyBusinessBenefit.
