# Product Brief

## Objective
Build a standalone Electron desktop application that automates high-quality backlink acquisition through an agentic, auditable, self-improving workflow.

## North-star workflow
DISCOVER → QUALIFY → RESEARCH → FIND CONTACT → PERSONALIZE → OUTREACH → FOLLOW UP → INTERPRET REPLY → NEGOTIATE → ACQUIRE → VERIFY → REPORT → LEARN → OPTIMIZE → REPEAT

## Users
SEO/digital marketing operators, outreach/content teams, campaign managers, and management/executives.

## Initial hard quality policy
DA > 45 and Spam Score < 1, configurable by campaign. Exact proprietary metrics remain provider-specific.

## Illustrative impact targets
- 70%+ reduction in manual operational effort.
- 3–10× more qualified prospects evaluated.
- 50–90% lower internal cost per verified backlink.
- 4–6 month economic payback.
- improved search visibility and organic traffic over time.

## Non-goals
Paid per-token AI dependency, black-hat/spam link building, uncontrolled mass email, autonomous publishing to third-party sites, bypassing provider authentication, or silent self-promotion of learned policies.
